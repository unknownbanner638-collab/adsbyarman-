Frequently asked questions

Honest answers before you get started.

What is Meta Ads lead generation?

It is the use of paid ads on Facebook and Instagram to reach people who may be interested in your offer and invite them to contact you, for example through a WhatsApp message or a form.

Can you generate affiliate marketing leads?

Yes, we run campaigns designed to attract inquiries for affiliate offers. Results depend on your offer, audience, budget and market, so we cannot guarantee a specific number of leads, sales or profits.

How does your campaign setup service work?

We discuss your business, plan the strategy and targeting, set up and launch the campaign, then monitor performance and optimize based on the data available.

What budget is required to run Meta Ads?

There is no single right number. Your ad budget depends on your goals and market, and it is paid to Meta separately. Share your range with us and we will suggest a realistic starting point.

Can I target a specific audience?

Yes. Meta Ads lets you target by location, age, interests and behaviors. We help you research and choose the audience that best fits your offer.
